Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: we only collect what the app needs to plan your trips, keep your journal and run your account. We never sell your data. Your journal is private unless you choose to share it. You can download or delete everything at any time from Account.

1. Who we are

SpeedCurrency (the website and the travel-planner app at /app) is run by David Megameno Nanyemba, trading as SpeedCurrency, Ondangwa, Namibia ("we", "us"). Contact for anything about your privacy: gmega.url@gmail.com.

3. Who processes your information for us

We use trusted service providers who process data only to run SpeedCurrency:

  • Supabase – database, file storage and sign-in (servers in London, United Kingdom).
  • Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude – generates assistant answers. Your question, plus the profile details needed to tailor the answer (home country, nationality, preferred currency), is sent to the AI provider for each answer. While we use Google's free Gemini service, Google may use these questions and answers to improve its products, so please don't include private details in assistant questions.
  • Google AdSense – shows ads to Free-plan users (see section 6).
  • GoatCounter – privacy-friendly visitor counts.
  • GitHub Pages – hosts the website files.
  • OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap / Photon and Overpass – map tiles, place search and nearby places. The places you search are sent to these services.
  • Wikipedia, Wikivoyage and Wikimedia Commons – town stories, travel guides and photos of places. The town name or the map point of the place you open is sent to them.
  • Open-Meteo – weather for featured towns (only town coordinates are sent, never your own location).
  • Your device's location – only if you tap the "locate me" button on a map and allow it. It is used on your device to centre the map and is not stored.
  • Voice search – if you tap the microphone, your browser's own speech service (for example Google in Chrome) turns your voice into text. We receive only the text.
  • Have I Been Pwned – when you choose a new password, only the first 5 characters of a scrambled (hashed) version are sent to check for leaked passwords. Your password never leaves your device.
  • Google News – the Updates newspaper. The place and topic you search for are sent to Google News to find articles.
  • Currency rate feed – exchange rates (no personal data is sent).

Some providers are outside Namibia. We choose providers with strong security and data-protection commitments.

4. Who can see what

  • Everything is private by default.
  • Journal entries become visible to others only if you switch on "Public journal" and mark the entry public.
  • Other users can see your name, username and bio when you message, comment or share publicly.
  • We look at a proof-of-payment file only to approve that payment, and at reported content only to handle the report.
  • We will disclose information if Namibian law or a court order requires it.
  • We never sell or rent your personal information.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, journal, trips and chats: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Payment records: for as long as Namibian tax and accounting law requires, even after account deletion (your proof-of-payment file is deleted with your account; the payment record itself is kept). They are then deleted.
  • Reports about abuse: up to 12 months.

6. Cookies, local storage and ads

The app stores your sign-in session and small preferences in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. It also keeps your Journey Pulse progress (XP, streak, quests, badges) and your ♥ Saved places only on your device; they are not sent to us and are removed if you clear your browser data. Free-plan users see ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show and measure ads. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can manage ad personalisation at Google Ad Settings and read how Google uses data from sites that use its services. Premium and Pro users see no ads.

7. Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • See and download your data: Account → "Download my data".
  • Correct your profile in Account.
  • Delete your account and all your content: Account → "Delete account". This cannot be undone.
  • Ask us any question, object to a use of your data, or complain: gmega.url@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.

Namibia's Constitution (Article 13) protects your privacy. We follow internationally recognised data-protection principles (similar to the EU GDPR and South Africa's POPIA) and will update this policy when Namibia's data protection law comes into force.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database rules make sure each account can only read its own private data. Photos and proof-of-payment files are stored privately and shared only through short-lived links. Optional two-step sign-in is available in Account. If a breach affects your information, we will tell you without undue delay.

9. Children

SpeedCurrency is for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes

If we make important changes, we will show a notice in the app before they take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
